01 — Brand
Identity & Depth
Design Philosophy
"Deep space, but alive. The violet of a city at night, with something quietly organic beneath."
Spectre takes the electric precision of V1 and grounds it with the faintest trace of the forest beneath. The grid, the glows, the violet rings — all V1. But at the outermost edge of the orb, the faintest teal-green bleeds in, like moss on stone. Technology rooted in something living.
Dominant
Electric violet. The city at night, neon on wet pavement, something vast and signal-rich.
Whisper
Forest teal at the periphery only. Roots beneath the signal. Barely visible, deeply felt.
Personality
Precise, not clinical
Electric, not anxious
Organic under the surface
Gold for the rare moment only
Urban, nocturnal, alive
The Grove Rule
Teal-green appears only at the outermost orb ring, in the Log section headers, and as one label accent. It is felt, not seen. If you notice it immediately, there's too much.

Violet — 75%
Signal violet leads everything. Backgrounds, borders, glows, the orb's inner rings, all buttons, active states. The hue is pure V1 — electric without being aggressive, technological without being cold.
Grove — 25%
Grove teal appears only at the edges — the outermost orb ring, section labels, one Log header accent. It shifts the whole palette from pure electronic to something that breathes. Invisible unless you look for it.
Amber — The Moment
Against deep violet-black, amber is devastating. The mutual wave dot glows gold and the entire dark screen responds. Maximum emotional impact. Used nowhere else — not in buttons, not in labels, not in nav.

07 — Growth
Virality
01
Passive Trigger Loop
Physical movement is the trigger. No app open required. The loop runs silently. Users return to discover who was near them — the city as a log.
02
48h Text Window
After a mutual Wave, a text channel opens for 48 hours only. Urgency without permanence. Forces quality over accumulation. The amber glow fades.
03
Encounter Card Share
"My signal reached 31 people." Deep violet-black. One amber number. One grove accent line. The app markets itself through the beauty of its own moments.
04
Physical QR
No referral codes. A violet-on-void QR card for stickers and posters. Leave a signal everywhere you go. The city is the medium.
